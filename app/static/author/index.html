Interview brief, survey & hypotheses

Write this shortly before the call. Saves to context/brief.json, context/survey.json and context/hypotheses.json on this machine only -- all three are git-ignored and never committed.

Brief

What you know or suspect about the interviewee. Every item needs an explicit evidence/inference choice below -- there is no default, on purpose (CTX-02): a guess must never be presented to the coach as fact.

Survey

The ordered list of questions you plan to ask. The coach tracks which ones you actually asked, from your own microphone -- never from a suggestion shown, whether or not you followed it.

Hypotheses

What you believe about this customer, stated so it can be proven wrong. Every hypothesis needs an explicit falsification condition -- what evidence would make it FALSE -- and this page will not save one without it, on purpose: a hypothesis nobody could ever disprove is not one the coach reading it next can act on.
